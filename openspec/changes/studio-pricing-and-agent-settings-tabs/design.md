## Context

Three pages each have their own copy of the same left rail: `routes/Studio.tsx`, `routes/Pricing.tsx` and `routes/admin/AgentSettings.tsx`. Each holds a `TABS` array, a `role="tablist"` rail from `components/chat/Rail.tsx` and a `Page` with its own `<h1>`. `App.tsx` lists "Pricing Overview" in `links` and "AI & Agent Settings" in `adminLinks`. It computes `dev` (the developer probe through `api.models()`) and gates `/admin/*` with `signedIn && dev`, sending everyone else to `/pieces`.

`studio-tile-grid` (in progress) adds `components/studio/SubMenu.tsx`, the `/studio/:tab/*` route, `Page wide` and the measured `--studio-offset` to `Studio.tsx`. This change is built on top of that work and is applied only after it is archived (owner decision, 2026-10-14).

`routes/Conversation.tsx` (about 1,000 lines) is one component: the panel `Rail` (Tools, Decisions so far, Brief, Project, Piece, Room flyouts) at the left edge, a chat column sized by a measured `--chat-h`, and at most one side `aside` (piece `InspectorView` from `?inspect=`, or `RoomView` from `?room=`, never both) in a fixed `lg:grid-cols-2` split. `routes/Conversations.tsx` lists conversations and has "New conversation" (`api.createThread`). `Pieces.tsx` links to `/conversations/<id>`.

Owner decisions (2026-10-14): the AI & Agent Settings tab is developer-only and keeps its `/admin/agents/*` addresses; both header links are removed and the old addresses forward; rail order is Pieces, Measured Rooms, Model Library, Pricing, then a divider and AI & Agent Settings; the page title stays "Design Studio". Workspace: a first rail tab whose sub-menu lists conversations; three layouts chosen from a layout button and menu at the right of the title row; drag handles with double-click reset; Chat / Piece / Room tabs on a phone; the conversation's panel icons in the chat pane header; all of this in this change rather than a separate one.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** one rail and one sub-menu for all six tabs; the Pricing and agent-settings view bodies moved without changing their logic, queries or refusals; the developer gate and the separate developer route tree unchanged; the conversation split into panes with its turn, stream, confirmation, edit, stop and decision logic unchanged.

**Non-Goals:** a tile grid or dialogs for pricing or agent settings; changes to any view's content, the inspector or the room 3D view; changes to Guidelines, Knowledge, Rules or Help; layouts saved per conversation or on the server; more than one conversation on screen at once; any backend change.

## Decisions

### Tabs, Pricing and AI & Agent Settings

- **Tab list carries the sub-menu.** Each entry in `Studio.tsx`'s `TABS` gains `views: {id, label, icon?}[]`, `base` (its address prefix: `/studio/<id>`, or `/admin/agents` for the developer tab), and `dev?: true`. The rail filters out `dev` tabs when `dev` is false and puts a `<div role="separator">` before the first `dev` tab. Pricing's and AgentSettings' existing `TABS` arrays become those `views` and their `body` map. One table drives the rail, the sub-menu and the body. *Alternative:* keep `Pricing.tsx` and `AgentSettings.tsx` as pages wrapped by Studio. Rejected, because that keeps three rails' worth of code.
- **Pricing and AgentSettings become tab bodies.** `Pricing.tsx` exports `PRICING_VIEWS` and `PricingTab({ view })`, and `AgentSettings.tsx` exports `AGENT_VIEWS` and `AgentSettingsTab({ view })`. Each renders only the chosen view's body, with no rail, `Page` or `<h1>`, and falls back to the first view for an unknown id by `<Navigate replace>` to that view's address. The file names stay, so the git history of their bodies stays readable.
- **Developer addresses render the Studio.** `App.tsx` keeps the `/admin/agents` and `/admin/agents/:tab` routes and their `signedIn && dev` guard, but their element becomes `<Studio dev fixedTab="agents" />`. `Studio` takes `fixedTab` in place of `useParams().tab` and reads the view from `:tab`. The route tree, the gate and the non-developer redirect stay exactly where they are, which keeps CLAUDE.md §1 ("separate routes") true. *Alternative:* `/studio/agents/*` with a gate inside the Studio. Rejected by the owner.
- **Owner routes.** `/studio/pricing/*` is served by the existing `/studio/:tab/*` route, and the rest of the path is the view. `/pricing` and `/pricing/:tab` become `<Navigate replace>` to `/studio/pricing/<tab>`. `/price-list`, `/snapshots` and `/remnants` point straight at `/studio/pricing/<view>`, without a chain through `/pricing`. An unknown `/pricing/<x>` lands on `/studio/pricing/<x>`, which falls back to Prices.
- **Header.** "Pricing Overview" is dropped from `links` and "AI & Agent Settings" from `adminLinks`. The "Design Studio" `NavItem` is current when the path starts with `/studio` or `/admin/agents`. It uses `useLocation` for that, because `NavLink`'s own `isActive` only matches its own `to`.
- **`dev` reaches the Studio as a prop** (`<Studio dev={dev} />`), from the probe `App` already runs. The Studio makes no probe of its own and adds no context. A non-developer never gets the developer tab in the rail, and `App`'s guard already keeps them off its addresses.
- **Layout.** All tabs use the Studio's `Page wide`. Pricing and agent-settings views are not grid views, so they ignore `--studio-offset`, scroll normally, and sit in a `max-w-5xl` box: the width they have today. The phone sub-menu row scrolls sideways (`overflow-x-auto`, as in `studio-tile-grid`), so the eight developer views fit at 360 px.

### Workspace

- **Split the conversation by extraction, not rewrite.** `Conversation.tsx` keeps its state and handlers (`send`, `answer`, `stop`, `follow`, `inspect`, `inspectRoom`, `addDecision`, and so on) in a `useConversation(threadId)` hook moved out of the component unchanged. The JSX splits into `ChatPane` (header with title, delete and panel icons, messages, composer), `PiecePane` (the former inspector `aside` body) and `RoomPane` (the former room `aside` body). They stay in `Conversation.tsx` beside the helper components they use (Tools, the panels, Title, DeleteConversation), so those move nowhere. The `aside`s' close buttons are dropped. `routes/Workspace.tsx` holds the hook and arranges the panes. *Alternative:* render the old page inside the Studio and add panes around it. Rejected, because the piece and room panes must be siblings of the chat for layout 3.
- **Pane content resolution.** Piece pane: `parseInspect(?inspect)` if valid, else `progress.package_id` at its latest version, else the "no piece yet" text. Room pane: `parseRoom(?room)` when its slug is the progress room, else the progress room at its latest version, else the "no room" or "no 3D view yet" texts the Project panel already words. The current `roomShown = !inspected && …` coupling is removed. `inspect()` and `inspectRoom()` keep writing the address, and additionally set the layout to `chat-piece` or `chat-room` when the current layout lacks that pane (on a phone they choose the pane's tab instead).
- **Resizing: `react-resizable-panels` v4 through a shadcn-style `Resizable` wrapper** (`ResizablePanelGroup`, `ResizablePanel`, `ResizableHandle withHandle` over v4's `Group`, `Panel` and `Separator`). It brings dragging, keyboard arrows with `role="separator"` and `aria-valuenow`, `minSize="20%"`, and a double-click on a handle that restores its panels' default sizes. Sizes are remembered per group with `useDefaultLayout({ id, storage })`. Group ids are `workspace.chat-piece`, `workspace.chat-room`, `workspace.piece-room.rows` and `workspace.piece-room.cols`. The library's storage is wrapped by a `storage` object whose `getItem` and `setItem` sit in `try/catch`, so a blocked store falls back to defaults.
- **Panes keep their state across layouts.** Each layout is a different tree, so rendering a pane inside it would remount the pane on every switch, losing a half-typed message, the scroll position and the 3D camera. Each pane is instead rendered once, with `createPortal`, into its own element, and a layout's `Slot` moves that element into place. Only the panes the layout (or the phone tab) shows are rendered, so no hidden 3D view runs. *Alternative:* a hand-made drag splitter. Rejected, because it would need its own pointer-capture, keyboard and ARIA code.
- **Layout choice.** `useWorkspaceLayout()` (`lib/workspaceLayout.ts`) keeps `"chat-piece" | "chat-room" | "piece-room"` under `studio.workspace.layout` with the same `try/catch` read and write as `useRailLabels`. It is a small shared store, so the Studio's layout menu and the Workspace see the same choice at once. `useRailLabels` likewise tells every mounted rail when the switch changes, so the chat header's icons follow the Studio rail. It defaults to `chat-piece`. The layout is not put in the address: it is the owner's preference, not part of what a link shows.
- **Layout button and menu.** It uses shadcn `DropdownMenu` (`@radix-ui/react-dropdown-menu`), a trigger with the `LayoutPanelLeft` icon and `aria-label="Layout"`, and a `DropdownMenuRadioGroup` of the three layouts, each with a 32 × 20 inline SVG pictogram. Radix gives focus handling, Escape and `aria-checked`. The Studio title row becomes `flex justify-between`, and the tab body passes its action through a `titleAction` slot that only the Workspace tab fills.
- **Height.** The Workspace replaces `--chat-h` with the Studio's measured `--studio-offset` from `studio-tile-grid`, so the outer group is `h-[calc(100dvh-var(--studio-offset))]`. Each panel is `overflow-auto`. The chat pane keeps its inner messages box with its own scroll and the "stay at the last message" logic.
- **Panel icons in the chat header.** `Rail`'s flyout logic is reused. The icon list renders horizontally in the chat pane header (a `variant="row"` prop on `Rail`), and the flyout is positioned over the chat pane (`absolute` within the pane, which is `relative`). Labels follow `useRailLabels`, so the rail switch shows them too.
- **3D views while resizing.** `InspectorView` and `RoomView` draw through `InspectorCanvas`, a `@react-three/fiber` `Canvas`. The Canvas measures its own container with a ResizeObserver, so it follows a pane resize without a window `resize` event and needs no change. Its fixed height (`h-[50vh]` in the compact inspector, `h-[60vh]` in the room view, both `min-h-80`) is kept. The Rooms page and the inspector page share these components, and a pane shorter than its view scrolls, as each pane does. *Alternative:* fill the pane's height. Deferred: it would change the shared components and is not needed for the layouts to work.
- **Phone.** `useMediaQuery("(min-width: 1024px)")` from `studio-tile-grid` switches between the panel groups and a `role="tablist"` with Chat, Piece and Room. Only the chosen pane is mounted, so one WebGL context is alive on the phone. 1024 px is used rather than the sub-menu's 768 px, because between 768 and 1024 the rail and the sub-menu column leave under 600 px for two panes.
- **Routes.** `/studio/workspace` and `/studio/workspace/:threadId` are served by `/studio/:tab/*`. `/studio/workspace` with no id does `<Navigate replace>` to the newest thread from `api.threads`. `/conversations` and `/conversations/:threadId` become `<Navigate replace>` keeping `location.search`. `Conversations.tsx` is deleted, because its list and "New conversation" now live in the Workspace sub-menu (`useQuery(["threads"])`, `api.createThread` and then navigate). Its per-row delete is not carried over, because a conversation is deleted from its own chat pane header (the "Conversations are deleted from the list" requirement already names the conversation view).

- **Tests.** jsdom lays nothing out. The shared test setup gives elements inside a resizable group a size, measured off-screen so that user-event's clicks at (0, 0) are not taken for handle drags, and a `ResizeObserver` that reports each element's size once.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [A developer bookmark to `/admin/agents/...` now shows a page titled "Design Studio".] → This is the title the owner chose. The rail and sub-menu mark the chosen tab and view.
- [`studio-tile-grid` changes `Studio.tsx` and `SubMenu` before this change is applied.] → This change is applied only after that one is archived. Task 1.1 checks the base before any edit.
- [Losing the per-page `aria-label` tablists ("Pricing Overview", "AI & Agent Settings") breaks tests that query them.] → Tests are moved to the Studio's tablist and the sub-menu `nav`. The scenarios stay the same, and only the queries change.
- [A wider `Page` makes the price list table and the Models page stretch on a laptop.] → The Pricing and AI & Agent Settings tab bodies are wrapped in a `max-w-5xl` box. That matches the width the views have today, so they look the same. The browser check covers both widths.

- [Layout 3 on a 1280 × 800 laptop leaves each 3D view roughly 500 × 380 px.] → The minimum size is 20 %, and the owner can drag the chat down or switch layouts. The browser check records whether the views stay usable.
- [Two WebGL contexts (piece and room) in layout 3 on a CPU-only laptop.] → Only the panes of the current layout are mounted, and each disposes its renderer on unmount, as today.
- [Extracting `useConversation` from a large component risks behaviour drift.] → The hook is moved without changing its body. Every existing `Conversation.test.tsx` assertion is kept and pointed at the Workspace, and none is weakened.
- [`react-resizable-panels` storage keys and `studio.workspace.layout` can hold stale values after a layout change in a later release.] → The library validates stored sizes against the panel count and falls back to defaults. An unknown layout id falls back to `chat-piece`.

## Migration Plan

Frontend only, with no data change. Two new frontend dependencies (`react-resizable-panels`, `@radix-ui/react-dropdown-menu`) are added with `pnpm add`, and `pnpm-lock.yaml` is committed. Rollback is a revert of the change's commits. Old bookmarks keep working through the redirects.
