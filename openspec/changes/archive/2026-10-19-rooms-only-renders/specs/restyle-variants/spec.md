## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: A look applies per slot and never changes the spec
**Reason**: Renders are made of room versions only (owner, 2026-10-18); a piece is not restyled.
**Migration**: A piece's colours and finishes are changed by a revision with the Designer. A room is still restyled with a style preset.

### Requirement: Keeping a look goes through the normal revision
**Reason**: There are no piece variants to keep a look from.
**Migration**: Ask the Designer for the material change directly.

### Requirement: A piece variant is a rateable render
**Reason**: Piece variants are no longer made, and existing ones were deleted on 2026-10-18.
**Migration**: None.
